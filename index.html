My dearest, most beautiful Mum,

There are not enough words in any language to tell you how much you mean to me — but I will spend my whole life trying.

Thank you for being the kind of mum who makes every room feel safer, every problem feel smaller, and every ordinary day feel like something worth treasuring.

Thank you for the late nights you stayed up worrying about me. For every meal made with love. For every time you believed in me even when I didn't believe in myself. For every hug that fixed everything.

I am who I am because of you. Your strength, your grace, your warmth — it lives in me. And I carry you with me everywhere I go.

I am so incredibly proud and lucky to be your daughter. Being yours is the greatest honour of my life.

On this day and every day — I love you more than you will ever know.
