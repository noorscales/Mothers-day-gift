# Creative brief — Mother's Day short

Source material: `../index.html` and `../mothers-day.pdf` (the interactive gift page).

**Audience:** one person — Mum. Secondary: friends/family if she shares it.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 30–45 s, voice-over + kinetic captions.
**Voice:** a daughter speaking; warm, intimate, a little playful, never salesy.

## Facts the script may use (from the gift page)
- A bouquet of roses — "each rose is a love note — timeless, beautiful, deeply fragrant."
- A luxury bouquet wrapped in Dior.
- A Hermès Birkin — "because you are, after all, the most iconic woman I know."
- Chloé Eau de Parfum — "soft, feminine, unforgettable … fills every room with warmth."
- A letter: "Being your daughter is my greatest privilege. You gave me strength,
  kindness, and a love that knows no end."
- Sign-off: "Forever your little girl." / "Send a kiss!"

## Visual palette
Blush `#FFB6C1`, rose `#FF6B8A`, hot pink `#FF1493`, crimson `#DC143C`, cream `#FFF0F3`.
Serif display type, falling petals, soft gradients.

## Allowed scene visuals
`roses`, `bouquet`, `bag`, `perfume`, `letter`, `hearts`, `title`
