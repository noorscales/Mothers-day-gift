# Creative brief — trading reel: "Risk first"

**Audience:** new and struggling retail traders scrolling Reels/Shorts/TikTok.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 30–40 s, voice-over + kinetic captions + animated charts.
**Voice:** an experienced trader; calm, direct, zero hype. Educational, not advice.
**Promise:** in under 40 seconds, understand why position sizing beats entry picking.

## Core ideas (all maths must be correct)
- Most accounts blow up from oversized positions, not from bad entries.
- **1% rule:** risk at most 1% of the account per trade.
  - 10 straight losses at 1% risk leave ~90.4% of the account (0.99^10).
  - 10 straight losses at 10% risk leave ~34.9% (0.9^10).
- **Stop-loss first:** decide the exit before entering, not after it hurts.
- **Risk:reward of at least 1:2.**
  - At 1:2 you can lose 6 of 10 trades and still finish +2R
    (4 wins × 2R = +8R, 6 losses × 1R = −6R).
- Close: protect the downside and the upside takes care of itself.

## Compliance
- On-screen "Not financial advice · Educational only" for the whole reel.
- No tickers, no predictions, no income claims.

## Visual palette
Navy `#0B1020`, grid `#1C2541`, profit green `#22C55E`, loss red `#EF4444`,
accent amber `#F5B700`, text `#E6EDF7`. Sans-serif (Inter), monospace numbers.

`crash` (candles dumping), `risk` (1% slice of the account), `drawdown`
